Fragment handoff check (SIG-7)

Does a link fragment survive email, link scanners and chat apps? Send a probe link through a channel, open it on the other side, and this page reports whether the fragment arrived intact. The key in the link is a dummy made for this test, not a real key. Nothing on this page sends data anywhere.
